Social Poster for TikTok - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Social Poster is a personal, self-hosted tool that runs only on Michael Herington's own computer. Michael uses it solely to post his own content (RedLine Survival, Leaf & Lore Coloring Books, and Agent Prompts A-Z) to his own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and no other people sign in to it.

What data it uses

  • The TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens for Michael's own TikTok account.
  • Basic profile information for that account (open ID and display name), used only to show which account is connected.
  • The videos and captions Michael chooses to upload to his own account.
  • No data about any other TikTok user is collected, stored, sold, or shared.

Storage and security

Tokens and profile information are stored only on Michael's own computer, in files readable by his user account alone. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API. The tool has no advertising or analytics, and data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services (Manage app permissions). Locally stored tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected from the tool.
