Leaf & Lore Poster - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Leaf & Lore Poster is a private scheduling tool used only by Leaf & Lore Coloring Books (published by Spirited Goat Coffee House) to publish pins of our own coloring book artwork to our own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to the public, and no other people sign in to it.

What data it uses

  • Our own Pinterest account's access token, boards list, and the pins we create, used only to post and manage our own content.
  • No personal data from other Pinterest users is collected, stored, sold, or shared.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer in a file readable by our account alone. We do not run advertising or analytics in this tool, and we do not share data with third parties.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest settings (Apps), which deletes the tool's ability to post. Locally stored tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected.
